## Purpose and effect

This Contributor License Agreement ("Agreement") is between the contributor identified in the applicable acceptance record and Orime, Inc. A recipient of the Project obtains rights under the license actually provided with that distribution or under a separate agreement. This CLA is not an independent unrestricted license for all recipients to use the Project, remove its branding, or operate an external commercial platform. Accepting the CLA does not itself grant You a partnership or platform-operation authorization.

This Agreement does not revoke rights already granted in earlier software distributions and does not amend another agreement merely because the Company publishes a new document. Any prior agreement continues to govern the contributions it covers unless the relevant parties expressly agree otherwise.

## Acceptance and version record

### Individual acceptance

Read the exact version linked by the Project's signing process. For acceptance through GitHub, the required comment is:

> I have read and agree to the Breatic Contributor License Agreement version 2.0, including my contributions to this pull request.

The acceptance record must identify Your GitHub account, the acceptance comment and pull request, acceptance time, and this agreement version together with an immutable document reference or content hash. An unrelated comment, a previous-version signature, or the appearance of Your account in a contributor list is not acceptance of this version.

An acceptance comment using that sentence expressly includes Your Contributions already submitted to the pull request in which You post it, as identified by the pull request URL and its revision or commit range at acceptance. This Agreement also covers Your Contributions intentionally submitted after acceptance, subject to any later effective notice ending coverage of future submissions. Other earlier submissions are included only when You expressly identify and include them in the acceptance record. The current-pull-request inclusion applies to both a first contribution and an older pending pull request; it does not include unrelated pull requests, other authors' rights, or corporate rights You lack authority to grant.

If an employer or another entity owns relevant rights, obtain its authorization or an applicable Corporate agreement. Do not post private corporate addresses, signed forms or confidential rights documents in a public GitHub comment.

### Corporate acceptance

An authorized representative completes and signs the Corporate agreement and its schedules. Return it through the private contact route confirmed by the Company. Each authorized contributor is identified in Schedule A and completes the required individual acceptance so the Project can match contributions to the applicable rights records. Corporate Contributions submitted before corporate acceptance must be expressly included in Schedule B if they are to be covered. An employee's current-pull-request acceptance does not by itself include those corporate rights.

Electronic acceptance may be used where the signatory's authority, version and express consent can be reliably recorded. This text does not represent that an unsigned company form has been accepted.
