hevc_open.mp4

320x240 HEVC (libx265, open GOPs, B-frames) at 60000/1001 fps with AAC; 180 frames, 6 keyframes.
Made with FFmpeg 8.0.1 for the sample set's seek check, which reads every grid frame through the design's seek rule (no -ss below the earliest keyframe packet time plus the largest keyframe interval, landing check, doubling back-off) and compares it with a whole-file decode through the same placement chain; the exact generating command was not recorded with the sample.

From the maintainers' frame-grid sample set (inner#1306), copied unchanged. Used by container/__tests__/frame-grid-*.node.test.ts.
