Video packet timestamps of the 302 frame-grid samples (screen and canvas recordings made with Chromium
MediaRecorder, synthetic display-cadence and stall recordings, FFmpeg-encoded constant-rate, jittered, NTSC,
Matroska and AVI files), read with ffprobe 8.0.1:

  ffprobe -v error -of json -select_streams v:0 \
    -show_entries stream=r_frame_rate,avg_frame_rate,time_base:packet=pts,dts <file>

Per file: rFrameRate and avgFrameRate as reported (null when 0/0), ticksPerSecond = 1/time_base, and the
packet times in packet order as firstTick plus tickDeltas; a packet without pts is timed by its dts.
expectedFps and expectedPath are the maintainers' reference results for the frame-grid rate rule, produced
by an independent harness of the same rule and checked against the frames each rate keeps.
All samples were generated by the maintainers; no third-party media.
